The signature move

A constellation he draws himself

  1. Paint the wall in Ink-Cap Indigo; let it cure 48 hours before any pen work.
  2. Plot the rings in chalk — string + pushpin as a compass, lightest touch. Big ring over the headboard, two small ones drifting toward the corner.
  3. Connor inks the glyphs in gold paint pen — his runes, his rules. Pencil guides first if he wants; WHA panels open on the desk for reference.
  4. Dot the stars — scattered gold points between the rings; wipe the chalk with a dry cloth once the pen has set.
  5. Leave one ring unfinished. Atelier rule: there is always a next spell to learn.

Why it works: in Witch Hat Atelier, magic is drawn, not cast — which makes a kid with a pen the most powerful person in the room. The wall says exactly that. And if taste changes at 15: one coat of indigo, blank sky, new constellation.
