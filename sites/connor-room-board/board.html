An Ink & Grain design · Chapter 01

Connor’s Room

A room for Connor, 12 — reader, gamer, manga obsessive. Warm walnut furniture that will still be his at 16, drawn in the language of the page: ink lines, screentone, and one red spot of energy.

コナー
CONNOR design board
rev 05

The brief

Kid first. The old plan anchored the room on a giant media console — furniture for a TV, not for a twelve-year-old. This revision flips it: the TV keeps its wall but loses its furniture, and a real drawing-and-homework desk takes its place underneath. The walnut line stays; the hero changes.

01

Desk, not console

A 60″ walnut desk with built-in outlets under the wall-mounted TV — drawing station, homework base, game HQ.

02

Ink & screentone

Manga as graphic texture — black line, halftone shade, framed panel art. Never merch-wallpaper.

03

Room to grow

The fandom lives in swappable layers. At 16 the walnut is still great; only the posters change.

The paths not taken

Alternatives considered

Every design here beat a real rival. What was on the table, why it lost, and what won the spot.

passed onFirst direction

The media-console room

An 80″ Lafayette console anchoring the TV wall at $2,499 — furniture built around a television, not a twelve-year-old.

→ The Tate desk took the wall. Drawing beats watching.

passed onThe library, v1

Twin bookcase towers

Two Lafayette 36″ “bookcases” under the window — until the product photography revealed they stand 78″ tall. A bookcase that blocks the window isn’t a library.

→ The 30″ Tate console: window kept, $2,000 saved.

passed onLayout draft

Desk jammed in the window corner

The first draft packed desk, bed, and console into the north half and left a dead zone by the door — furniture huddling at one end of a 13′4″ room.

→ Desk centered under the TV, bed floated mid-wall: every piece gets air, and the light still lands on the drawing hand.

passed onClothes storage

A dresser in the room

Every open run measures just under either dresser: the door swing eats the south end of the desk wall, the bed wall’s free 3′5″ is well shy of the 62″ — and under the window it evicts the library. Neither trade is worth it.

→ Drawer system inside the mirrored closet, phase two.

passed onDesk option

The Lafayette desk

The line’s own desk comes only in whitewash — a pale stranger in a walnut room.

→ Tate 60″ in true walnut, outlets and USB built in.

passed onDesign language

The postered manga room

Merch wallpaper shouts, dates fast, and gets outgrown by fourteen.

→ Ink, screentone, one vermilion accent — manga as design language, in layers that swap.
